# jollof notes

A small guide for cooking party jollof rice at home. Made for beginners who want a simple starting point.

## What you need
- 3 cups parboiled rice
- 1 tin tomato paste
- Fresh peppers and onions, blended

## Steps
1. Heat the oil, then fry the tomato paste until it darkens.
2. Add the blended peppers and onions and cook down.
3. Add your seasoning and the rice.
4. Cover and cook on low heat.
